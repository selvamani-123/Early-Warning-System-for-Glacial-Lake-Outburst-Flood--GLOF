# GLOF Early Warning System

## Project Overview

Develop a web-based Early Warning System for Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOF). The platform monitors glacial lake conditions, weather patterns, and water-level data to estimate flood risk and provide early warnings to authorities and nearby communities.

The system should have a modern disaster-management dashboard with real-time visualization, risk analytics, interactive maps, and alert notifications.

---

## Objective

Provide an intelligent monitoring platform capable of:

* Monitoring glacial lakes
* Displaying real-time environmental data
* Predicting flood risk levels
* Visualizing affected regions
* Generating alerts and warnings
* Supporting disaster preparedness

## Application Modules

### Dashboard

Display:

* Current Risk Level
* Lake Water Level
* Temperature
* Rainfall
* Recent Alerts
* Active Monitoring Status

Risk Levels:

* Green → Low
* Yellow → Moderate
* Orange → High
* Red → Critical

---

### Interactive Map

Features:

* Glacial Lake Locations
* Risk Zone Visualization
* Flood-Prone Areas
* Sensor Locations
* Emergency Shelters

Use Leaflet.js for map rendering.

---

### Prediction Module

Inputs:

* Rainfall
* Temperature
* Water Level
* Glacier Melt Rate

Outputs:

* Flood Probability (%)
* Risk Category
* Suggested Action

---

### Alert System

Generate alerts when risk exceeds threshold.

Alert Types:

* Information
* Warning
* Emergency

Delivery:

* Dashboard Notification
* Email
* SMS (future enhancement)

---

### Historical Analytics

Visualize:

* Flood Events
* Water Level Trends
* Rainfall Trends
* Prediction Accuracy

Use Chart.js.
